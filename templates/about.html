About the project

A clearer research workflow for brain MRI experiments.

NeuroScan AI is a Bachelor of Engineering capstone interface that pairs deep-learning classification with visual evidence. The product title remains unchanged; the interface now makes the analysis pipeline easier to inspect and present.

Core inference stack

Backend: Python, Flask, TensorFlow/Keras, OpenCV, Matplotlib, ReportLab.

Models: VGG16, EfficientNetB0, custom CNN, and ANN adapters with optional weighted soft voting.

Interface: HTML5, Tailwind CSS, responsive glass-panel layout, accessible status and evidence cards.

NeuroScan AI is a research and educational prototype. Heatmaps and boundary estimates are not clinical segmentation, and model outputs require qualified clinical review.
